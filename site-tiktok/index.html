What it is

Shorts Agent is a desktop tool that runs on my own computer. I stream gameplay from PlayStation 5 to my YouTube channel GTA VI Streams (channel link). The tool helps me turn those streams into content that is easier for viewers to watch:

  • YouTube Shorts — short vertical clips of the best moments of my own streams, with subtitles.
  • Organized episodes — my stream recordings get a clear title (e.g. “Walkthrough Gameplay – Part 3”), a description with chapters (timestamps), a thumbnail, and are added to a playlist on my channel.

Who uses it

Only me, the owner of the channel. The tool is not offered to the public, has no user accounts and no website login. It works only with the YouTube channel I own and authorize myself through Google sign-in (OAuth 2.0).

TikTok

The same Shorts can be sent to my own TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. I sign in with my TikTok account, the tool uploads the clip to my TikTok inbox as a draft, and I review it, pick a sound and publish it myself in the TikTok app. Nothing is posted to anyone else’s account.
